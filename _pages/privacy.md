This website does not use cookies, analytics or any other tracking, and it does not load fonts or scripts from third parties.

**Hosting.** The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com). When you open a page, GitHub necessarily processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver it. See the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

**Light and dark mode.** If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your own browser (local storage) so that the site remembers it. It is never sent to me or anyone else, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

**External links.** Links to other websites, such as arXiv or YouTube, are subject to the privacy policies of those sites.
